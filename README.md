## 1. Free Chief of Staff template

A coordinator bot for solo creators and small businesses. It reads your priorities from one source of truth (a GitHub repo or a doc), ranks the top 1 to 3, protects your time, and **drafts everything, never sending or posting without your say-so**. Failures turn into written rules through a 5-question postmortem.
